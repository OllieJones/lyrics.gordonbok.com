Windcalling

Live performance of an excerpt from the folk opera “Another Land Made of Water”, © 1979 Gordon Bok. Vocals: Combined choruses of the Camden Hills Regional High School and QuasiModal Chorus, all directed by Kim Murphy. Story and instrumental artists directed by Glenn Jenks at the Strom Auditorium, 2008, Rec. Rushmore DeNooyer

Sheet music for this song is available for SATB chorus through Timberhead Music.

Windcalling is recorded on the CD Windcalling
