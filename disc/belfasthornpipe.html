Belfast Hornpipe

©Traditional

        I learned this from a recording of the Dubliners sent to me by my psychology colleague Jerry Singer who, like me, suffers from the dual addiction to psychology and folk music. It can be found on The Dublin.ers, 15 Years On, Chyme Records, CH CP 1025). Ann plays the whistle, I hammer the dulcimer, and Gordon supplies a perceptively puckish guitar accompaniment. (ET)

Belfast Hornpipe is recorded on the album Minneapolis Concert
